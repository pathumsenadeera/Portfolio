# Code of Ethics

As a creator, developer, and maintainer of this project, I commit to the following ethical principles:

## 1. Respect for Users
I will prioritize the privacy, security, and well-being of the users who interact with the software I build. I will not knowingly introduce malicious code, dark patterns, or features that exploit user trust.

## 2. Honesty and Transparency
I will accurately represent my skills, the capabilities of this project, and any known limitations or risks. I will transparently communicate about data collection, usage, and sharing.

## 3. Inclusivity and Accessibility
I am committed to designing and building software that is accessible to everyone, regardless of their physical abilities, hardware, or geographic location. I will strive to make inclusive design choices.

## 4. Continuous Learning and Improvement
I acknowledge that technology and ethical standards evolve. I commit to continuously learning, listening to feedback, and improving both my technical skills and my ethical awareness.

## 5. Do No Harm
I will not use my technical abilities or this project to intentionally cause harm to individuals, communities, or the environment. I will strive to consider the broader societal impact of my work.
